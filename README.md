## About me

- **Background:** B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

## Why data engineering
TCS is my first job, and production support has shown me how a bank's data platform really runs: batch jobs, failed loads, reruns and reconciliation. I've seen what happens when pipelines break. Now I want to be on the other side, designing and building pipelines that are reliable from the start.
